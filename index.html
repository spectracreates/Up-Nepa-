        How the status is worked out

        Each phone counts once per area: only its latest report in the last 6 hours is used, and a report loses half its weight every hour. Reports sent from within about 3.5km of the area, with good GPS, count fully; others count less. Confirmed needs 3 GPS-verified neighbours with at least 3 in 4 agreeing. Your exact location is checked and then thrown away, never stored.
